# Code of conduct

Keep discussions, reviews, and issue reports focused on the project. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Give specific feedback on the work, assume good faith, and respect other people's privacy.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, and sharing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable in project discussions. Maintainers may remove such content or restrict participation in the repository.

Use GitHub's private reporting tools to report harassment or abuse. [How to report abuse on GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
